# PRESSLY — Company overview

**Your Clothes. Ready When Life Happens.**

PRESSLY proposes urgent on-site garment care for professionals, travellers, hotel guests and residents. A technician brings portable equipment to the customer rather than collecting garments for a conventional laundry round.

The mission is convenient, suitable care with transparent prices and informed consent. The vision is a dependable local pilot supported by trained technicians. Values are fabric safety, accessibility, accountability and honest claims.

The initial offer is Express Pressing, Steam Refresh and Express Stain Treatment. Pressing and refreshing do not replace full washing. Stain removal is never guaranteed. Rapid Wash remains an equipment-dependent future concept requiring independent safety and timing validation.

Revenue assumptions include garment/spot charges, an AED 5 service fee and potential hotel or office partnerships. The classroom prototype uses illustrative prices and does not validate commercial demand or unit economics.

The operating proposition is a proposed 30-minute arrival target in narrow eligible zones; pressing/refresh completion targets 60 minutes. A restricted immediate-order promotion waives only the service fee if completion is late. Field testing, staffing capacity, insurance, permits and legal review are prerequisites to real operation.

No customer research, operational performance or sustainability savings have been measured. Generated editorial imagery depicts the concept, not a real operating fleet or customer base.
