# Presentation outline

1. Introduce PRESSLY and the last-minute garment-care problem with a fictional meeting scenario.
2. Explain the difference between on-site care and laundry collection.
3. Introduce customers, service scope and qualified timing targets.
4. Explain competitor positioning and Business Model Canvas assumptions.
5. Summarise SDLC decisions and feasibility risks.
6. Demonstrate service pricing, six-step booking and explicit demo checkout.
7. Switch to the technician view; accept and progress the same order.
8. Return to customer tracking, inspect timestamped events, reload and submit feedback.
9. Explain personalisation and why local event history is not blockchain; describe future verifiable-record options honestly.
10. Show draft guarantee exclusions, cancellation and privacy controls.
11. Present executed tests and separately identify outstanding usability/deployment checks.
12. Conclude with validation needed before a real pilot: demand, safe capacity, fabric care, permits, insurance and sustainable economics.

Keep supporting report prose within the final brief's 3,000-word interpretation. These concise notes support the submission; diagrams and expanded downloadable implementation specifications are supplementary only if the actual brief allows that. No invented group-member allocation, surveys or test outcomes.
