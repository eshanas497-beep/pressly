# Analysis

## Requirements

Functional: navigate all screens; calculate service and fee totals; validate six-step bookings; save UUID orders; accept/decline capability-matched jobs; advance valid statuses; expose customer tracking/history; rebook; save profiles, feedback and local inquiries; cancel only before departure; apply scoped late-completion fee waivers.

Non-functional: keyboard access, visible focus, labelled forms, responsive layouts, useful errors, small dependency footprint, no credentials in source and honest privacy boundaries. Successful local save is required before confirming a booking. Browser storage is not authentication or cross-device infrastructure.

## Competitors and positioning

Illustrative categories: traditional shops offer specialist inspection but require travel or collection; collection platforms offer scheduled convenience; home care offers control and low cash cost but uses customer time. PRESSLY proposes urgent on-site finishing, with limited treatment scope. Named examples in the website comparison are classroom comparisons; current provider features and turnaround have not been independently verified.

Positioning-map inputs should use axes **on-site convenience** and **treatment breadth**. Hypotheses: PRESSLY high convenience/limited breadth; traditional cleaner lower immediate convenience/broader specialist care; collection platform medium convenience/broader washing; home care high location convenience/skill-dependent breadth. These are qualitative assumptions, not survey scores.

## Business Model Canvas

Partners: trained technicians, equipment suppliers, hotels/offices, future payment provider. Activities: assessment, treatment, routing, training and support. Resources: equipment, technician capacity, protocols and platform. Value: suitable care where the customer is. Relationships: self-service booking plus human assessment/support. Channels: web, concierge and workplace referrals. Segments: professionals, travellers and residents. Costs: compensation, transport, consumables, equipment, hosting, insurance and support. Revenues: care fees, service fee and proposed partnerships.

No invented interviews, survey outcomes, market statistics or validated financial results are used.
